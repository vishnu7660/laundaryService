Who We Are

Founded in 2020, PureWash has grown from a single neighbourhood laundry room into a full-service fabric-care brand trusted by thousands of families.

Our team of trained fabric specialists handles every garment with the care it deserves — using eco-friendly detergents, water-saving machines, and biodegradable packaging. We believe quality service should never come at the cost of the planet.
